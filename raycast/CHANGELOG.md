# Mash Changelog

## [Initial Version] - {PR_MERGE_DATE}

- Search Mash: see your latest chats on this machine, find past chats with your coding agents as you type, read the matching messages, and copy a message ID or passage.
- Search every source at once (this machine by words and by meaning, and your other machines) in one list that fills in as each answers, keeps rows in place once you move the selection, and offers to retry a machine that doesn't answer.
- Open a Codex thread in the ChatGPT app, or a Claude Code session in the Claude app, in the folder it ran in (Add to Claude for a session the app doesn't have yet).
- With the Mash desktop app installed (in `/Applications` or `~/Applications`), open a chat or the full search in the app and copy a `mash://` link.
- The latest chats leave out the subagent and fork chats your agents start; search includes their matches unless you turn that off in the extension's preferences.
- When Mash can't search, show its message with ways to fix it: try again, the troubleshooting guide, or set where Mash is.
